About us

Growing quality, nurturing community

Udosen Agro Farms is a family agricultural business in Ikot Abasi, Akwa Ibom State. We grow vegetables, keep healthy laying hens and raise catfish and tilapia, with every product handled carefully from farm to customer.

We believe in honest pricing, good quality and real relationships with the people we feed.
